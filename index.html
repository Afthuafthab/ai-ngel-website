A camera, a wrist band and sensors, thinking together.

AI.ngel watches over a patient or an elder and tells the concerned person only what matters. It is a prototype and not a diagnostic tool.

  • A cameraMounted on the ceiling above the chest, angled 45 degrees down at the face. It watches 24/7.
  • A wrist bandKeeps an eye on heart rate, SpO2 and wrist temperature.
  • Sensors for the drip line and urine bagLow drip bag and full urine bag, reported without a nurse checking. In development.

The camera looks at the face, not at the room.

It reads unusual movement and signs of pain, such as a hand held on the chest. Video and faces never leave the AI computer.

45°from the ceiling above the chest to the patient's face

Sensors feed the vitals in.

The wrist band, the drip line sensor and the urine bag sensor send their readings to the AI computer, all on your own Wi-Fi.

The AI computer decides.

It processes every input together, the camera and the sensors, and gives out alerts to suit the situation. It runs at the bedside, with no cloud processing.

No false panic.

Movement alone sends only a gentle alert. The alarm sounds when the camera sees something unusual and the vitals change too.

  1. Gentle alertThe camera sees movement. The vitals look normal.
  2. AlarmSomething unusual and the vitals change together.
  3. Alarm at onceThe vitals themselves turn critical.
